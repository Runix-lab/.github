# Runix Lab open learning

Free material for programmers who want to examine AI data rather than only
read about models. You can study independently or adapt the material under the
licenses below. No Runix account or subscription is required.

| Resource | Scope | Language | License |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [AI4S for Programmers](https://ai4s.runixcloud.io) | Antibody/protein data, provenance, normalization and QC; six runnable Python labs | Chinese; [English overview](https://ai4s.runixcloud.io/en/) | Text CC BY-SA 4.0; code MIT, in the [course repository](https://github.com/Runix-lab/ai4s-for-programmers) |
| [Embodied AI data foundations](embodied-ai.md) | Episodes, alignment, grouped evaluation and dataset review; one stdlib exercise | English | CC BY 4.0, including the inline exercise |

The embodied resource is an introductory guide, not a complete robotics course
or a release of an existing private curriculum. Hardware control, policy
training and deployment are outside its scope. Existing additional courses can
be added after checking their source, redistribution rights and runnable steps.

Third-party reading and datasets are linked, not relicensed. Corrections and
small, runnable additions are welcome; see [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
